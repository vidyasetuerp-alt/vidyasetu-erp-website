Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 July 2026

Website information

This static website does not use a database and does not directly store enquiry form submissions. When you choose to send an enquiry, the details you entered are passed to WhatsApp or your email application so you can review and send them.

Information you choose to share

You may choose to provide institution, contact, location and requirement details when requesting a demo, quotation or support. VidyaSetu Tech may use information you send to respond, recommend a product, provide support or prepare a quotation.

Third-party services

GitHub Pages may process technical request information when serving this website. WhatsApp, email providers and GitHub Releases operate under their own privacy terms.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently set marketing cookies or include a paid analytics service. This statement should be updated if analytics or additional third-party tools are later added.

Data security and retention

Do not include confidential student records, passwords or sensitive institutional data in a general sales enquiry. Contact VidyaSetu Tech for secure support guidance. Information sent directly to the company may be retained as reasonably needed to respond and support the business relationship.

Your choices

You may request correction or deletion of information you previously sent, subject to legal and operational requirements, by emailing info@vidyasetuerptech.com.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the website and services evolve. The latest version will be posted on this page.

This starter policy is general website information and should be reviewed for your actual business practices and applicable legal requirements before launch.
